## Support (home)

Choose a feeling, read curated verses, then use **Actions** to copy or save a
verse. When you need the full chapter, use **Read full chapter**—you will land
in Reader with the right book, chapter, and translation.

- Switch translation from the selector near the top.
- Use **Learn context** when you want a short summary alongside the verse list.
- Return to Support any time from the menu.

<Link to="/" className="font-semibold text-indigo-700 hover:text-indigo-600">
  Open Support
</Link>

## Search

Use **Guided** when you know the book and chapter, **Reference** for strings
like `John 3:16-18`, and **Keyword** when you only have topic words.

- Save a single verse or use batch save when multiple results are selected.
- Open a result in Reader to keep reading in chapter context.
- Your last search form and scroll position are restored for the session when
  you navigate away and back.

<Link
  to="/search"
  className="font-semibold text-indigo-700 hover:text-indigo-600">
  Open Search
</Link>

## Saved scriptures

Saved is organized by **book** first. Inside a book you can edit translation
and notes, remove entries, or use **Open in Reader** to jump to the matching
verse in chapter view.

<Link
  to="/saved"
  className="font-semibold text-indigo-700 hover:text-indigo-600">
  Open Saved
</Link>

## Reader

- Tap a verse to open actions: save, note, share, or bookmark.
- **Bookmark** stores your scroll position for the chapter so you can jump back
  with **Jump to last place**.
- **Share verse** creates a public link anyone can open.
- The app remembers your last book, chapter, translation, and scroll position
  for the session when you leave Reader and return from the menu.

## Troubleshooting

- If a page fails to load, use **Try again** and stay on the same route.
- If saving fails, check the network and retry once.
- If share does not open on your device, use **Copy Link**.
- If Reader looks wrong, open **Options** and reset reader settings.
