## Search

Use **Guided** when you know the book and chapter, **Reference** for strings
like `John 3:16-18`, and **Keyword** when you only have topic words.

- Save a single verse or use batch save when multiple results are selected.
- Open a result in Reader to keep reading in chapter context.
- Your last search form and scroll position are restored for the session when
  you navigate away and back.
