## Getting started

<TutorialStep title="1. Ground yourself in Support">
  Open Support from the menu and pick the feeling that best matches your
  moment. Curated verses appear quickly—use them for prayer, journaling, or
  simply reading aloud.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="2. Search with confidence">
  When you know a reference or a topic phrase, use Search. Switch between
  Guided, Reference, and Keyword modes depending on what you remember.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="3. Save and organize">
  Save verses from Support or Search, then open **Saved** to review by book,
  edit notes, or reopen passages in Reader.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="4. Read chapters with comfort options">
  Reader keeps book, chapter, and translation in sync with the address bar. Use
  **Options** to tune theme, typography, spacing, and width for longer reading
  sessions.
</TutorialStep>
