## Display settings (reminder)

Open the **menu → Display settings** for app-wide **dark mode**, **high contrast**,
and **text size**. Reader still has its own **Options** inside the reader for the
reading pane. See the **Display settings** section earlier in this tutorial for
detail.

## Prayer partners & lists (reminder)

Signed-in users can open **Prayer Partners** and **Prayer Lists** from the menu
to track people, notes, filters, streaks, and prayer sessions. Use the **?** help
on those pages for a quick refresher, or revisit the **Prayer partners & lists**
section above.

## Troubleshooting

- If a page fails to load, use **Try again** and stay on the same route.
- If saving fails, check the network and retry once.
- If share does not open on your device, use **Copy Link**.
- If Reader looks wrong, open **Options** and reset reader settings.
