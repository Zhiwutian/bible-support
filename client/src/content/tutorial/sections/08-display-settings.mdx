## Display settings

Open the **menu** (top right), then **Display settings** to adjust:

- **Dark mode** — darker surfaces across the app. Reader still has its own
  theme options inside **Reader → Options** for the reading pane.
- **High contrast** — stronger contrast for controls and general UI text.
- **Text size** — larger or smaller type across menus and most pages (this is
  separate from Reader font size in Options).

These choices apply to your **device/browser** (guest or signed in) the way
other app settings do. Tap the same menu entry again to change or preview
settings.
