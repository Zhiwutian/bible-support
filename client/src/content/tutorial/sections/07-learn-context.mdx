## Study online (from Support)

From an emotion’s scripture view, **Study online** offers links to the same
passage on **Bible.com** and **BibleGateway** in a new tab. Use them when you
want commentary, audio, or reading outside the app.

- Links use your **saved Bible translation** (KJV, ASV, or WEB) where the site
  supports it.
- Third-party sites have their own content policies and terms.

The in-app **Reader** is still the place for chapter-by-chapter reading,
bookmarks, and verse actions. **Full Context** (from bookmarks or deep links)
remains available for longer on-app explanations when present.
