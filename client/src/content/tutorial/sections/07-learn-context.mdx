## Learn context (from Support)

From an emotion’s scripture view, **Learn context** opens a **Full Context**
page for that passage. There you get:

- A **longer explanation** (summary and extended paragraphs) to read at your
  own pace, with source attribution when available.
- **Back to scripture** to return to the verse list you came from.
- **Read full chapter** opens the chapter in an external Bible site
  (BibleGateway) in a new tab—useful when you want the whole chapter outside
  the app’s Reader.

Full Context is **not** the same as **Reader**: Reader stays inside the app for
chapter-by-chapter reading, bookmarks, and verse actions. Use each where it
fits your moment.
