## Saved scriptures

**Step 1 — Book list:** Open **Saved** from the menu. You’ll see one card per
Bible book that contains saved verses; counts show how many verses are saved in
each book.

**Step 2 — Book detail:** Tap a book to see every saved verse for that book.
From here you can:

- Change **translation** for an entry (where the UI offers it).
- Edit your personal **note** per saved verse and save.
- **Remove** a verse from your collection when you no longer need it.
- Use **Open in Reader** to continue in chapter context at the right verse.

**Session restore:** Scroll position on the **Saved** list and on each **book
detail** page is restored for the browser tab session when you navigate away
and come back.
