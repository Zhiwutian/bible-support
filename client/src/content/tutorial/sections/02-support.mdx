## Support (home)

Choose a feeling, read curated verses, then use **Actions** to copy or save a
verse. When you need the full chapter, use **Read full chapter**—you will land
in Reader with the right book, chapter, and translation.

- Support categories are loaded from the server. If they fail to load, use
  **Try again** on the error state.
- Switch translation from the selector near the top (where available).
- Use **Study online** to open the passage on Bible.com or BibleGateway (see the
  **Study online** section below).
- Use **Previous** / **Next** to move between verses in a list.
- Return to Support any time from the menu.
