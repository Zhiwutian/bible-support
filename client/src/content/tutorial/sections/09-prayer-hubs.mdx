## Prayer partners & lists (signed in)

Prayer features require **sign in**. Use the menu to open **Prayer Partners**
or **Prayer Lists**.

<TutorialCallout>
  Use the **?** help button on those pages for quick reminders about filters,
  streaks, and reminders without leaving the screen.
</TutorialCallout>

### Prayer Partners

- **Add a partner** with name, prayer focus, and an optional **image URL**
  (must be a normal `http` or `https` link to a hosted image—not pasted
  base64).
- Open a partner to **edit details**, add **dated notes**, and see history.
- **Archive** or **delete** from the roster when appropriate (delete is
  permanent).
- **Filters** (roster): include archived, “needs update” windows, image/notes
  filters, and sort order.
- **Streaks & reminders** (hub card): list-based prayer streaks and optional
  daily reminder settings (timezone-aware).

### Prayer Lists

- **Create lists** to group partners for focused prayer (e.g. family, church,
  work).
- Open a list to **edit name/description**, **add or remove members**,
  **reorder** members, and **log a prayer session** with an optional note.
- **Filters** work like the partners hub: archived lists, “not prayed
  recently,” and sort.
